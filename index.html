FAQ

Common questions

Is this online or offline coaching?+

Training Sessions are Online/offline — in-person, at the gym, Worldwide. This is hands-on, real coaching. I correct your form in real-time, push you when you need it and adjust on the spot. Nothing replaces being physically present.

What happens after I submit my application?+

I personally review every application within 24 hours. I then reach out via WhatsApp for a quick 10-minute intro call to understand your goals better, confirm your slot and answer any questions. After that, your program is built and we start.

Is payment monthly or per session?+

Payment is discussed and confirmed after your application is reviewed. All packages are monthly. I collect payment directly — no online gateway needed. We discuss the schedule, confirm your slot and handle payment before your first session.

Is nutrition coaching included?+

Yes — I am an IFA certified Sports Nutritionist. Nutrition guidance is included in all packages. 1:1 Personal and Elite clients get a complete customized diet plan. Group clients get structured nutrition guidance. Your diet is built around your goal, body type and food preferences.

I'm a complete beginner — is this for me?+

Absolutely. Most of my clients start as beginners. The Group package is specifically designed for beginners — it builds your foundation, teaches you proper form and gets you consistent. You don't need any prior experience. Just show up willing to work.

What time slots are available?+

All sessions are in the evening between 5 PM and 11 PM. You pick your preferred 1-hour slot when you apply. Slots are limited and fill up fast — which is why I recommend applying early to secure your preferred time.
